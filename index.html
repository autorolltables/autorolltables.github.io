Category switching

With hover, resting the pointer on a category in the left nav opens it without clicking. Hover is the default.

Favorites & custom tables

Star any table to pin it to the Favorites category. You can also write your own: open Favorites and choose New custom table to add a title and a list of results.

No tables starred.

The two are cleared separately, so clearing favorites leaves your custom tables alone. Custom tables are the ones you wrote, and clearing them cannot be undone, so that button asks twice. A single table can also be removed from its own edit dialog.

Backup & transfer

Everything lives in this browser, so it is worth exporting a copy now and then, or to move to another device. The file holds your starred favorites, your custom tables and the current roll history.

Importing merges a file into what is already here, so nothing you have now is removed. Favorites and custom tables you already have are not duplicated; history entries are always added to the end of the list.

About

Auto Roll Tables is a free collection of random tables for tabletop RPGs. Everything runs in your browser: there is no account, no server and no tracking, and your favorites and settings never leave this device.

Developed by dangeratio. Report an issue on GitHub or Reddit.

Your stats

Everything you have rolled on this device, counted as you go. Kept in this browser like the rest of your data, and included in a backup so it survives a move.

Resetting clears the counts only. Your favorites, custom tables and history are untouched.
