About

Auto Roll Tables is a free collection of random tables for tabletop RPGs. Everything runs in your browser: there is no account, no server and no tracking, and your favorites and settings never leave this device.

Developed by dangeratio. Report an issue on GitHub or Reddit.

Attribution

The roll tables collected here were written and shared by the tabletop community. Enormous thanks to OrkishBlade and everyone at /r/BehindTheTables, whose tables make up the bulk of this site.
