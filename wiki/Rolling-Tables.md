# Rolling Tables

## Categories

Tables are grouped into five browsing categories, plus **All** and
**Favorites**.

| Category | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Characters** | NPCs by trade and station, and whole factions: guilds, cults, crews, warbands |
| **Locations** | Dungeons, castles, settlements from hamlets to cities, wilderness, weather |
| **Items** | Weapons, armour, potions, magic items, food and drink, coins, books, vehicles |
| **Creatures** | Beasts, dragons, golems, undead, fiends, animals |
| **Plots** | Campaign themes, adventure hooks, rumours, crimes, world-shaking events |
| **All** | Every table in one alphabetical list |
| **Favorites** | Tables you starred, plus your own custom tables |

Several categories merge more than one underlying source. Characters is
Factions and NPCs together, Locations is Dungeons, Settlements and Wilderness,
and Items is Food, Magic, Objects and Vehicles.

On desktop the categories are the left sidebar. On phones they are the bottom
tab row, with **More** holding All, Favorites, the map generators and Settings.

## Rolling

Click any table in the left list. The result appears under **Current Roll**.

A roll is usually a whole scene rather than one line. A table names a set of
sub-tables, all of which are rolled, and an individual result can itself contain
an inline roll that is resolved before you see it. So one click on a tavern
gives you the building, its keeper, its regulars and what is in the pot.

Some tables roll differently:

- **Pick tables** roll once to choose a list, then once on the list they landed
  on. *Random Plot Hook* works this way: it picks an environment, then draws a
  hook from it, and names the list it used.
- **Count tables** roll a quantity first, then that many results. The castle and
  city tables do this, which is why they are labelled with things like
  *(with 8d6 rooms)*.

Tables whose name begins with a dash are shown indented. They are variants or
components of the table above, and can be rolled on their own.

## Current Roll and History

**Current Roll** shows the most recent result. **History** keeps everything
rolled this session.

- **Expand** and **Collapse** open or close every entry in the history.
- The trash icon on an entry deletes that one entry.
- **Clear** empties the whole history.
- **Copy** copies the current roll; **Copy all** copies the entire history.

Copied output is plain text, ready to paste into notes or chat.

## Filtering

The filter box above the table list narrows the current category as you type.
It matches on table names. Clear it with the × button.

Filtering only searches the category you are in, so use **All** to search
everything at once.
